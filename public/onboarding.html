Welcome to loci

Everything you texted yourself.

loci turns your texts into notes. Send a thought to one number and it's saved, dated and searchable — no app to open, nothing to file. Tag a text and it becomes something more: a todo for today, a time block, a goal to keep track of.

  • parked in level 3, row F — a note
  • #todo book the dentist — today's list
  • #busy 9am-5pm work — a time block
  • #goals run a half marathon — a goal

Your notes are yours: they're written to a private folder in your own Google Drive, not kept on our servers.
