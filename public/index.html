How to use loci

Text your loci number to save a note, or write one below. Anything you send is saved as-is; a few tags turn a text into something more specific:

#todo <item>
Adds to today's list.
#done <what you finished>
Crosses an item off today's list.
#busy 9am-5pm work
Blocks time on the calendar — or "#busy 3pm for 2 hrs".
#goals <goal>
Tracks a goal; add "- subtask" lines under it.
#create list <name>
Makes a list from the lines that follow.
item #listname
Adds to an existing list.
#help
Texts these instructions back to you.
